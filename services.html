Primary Care Services

Comprehensive, whole-person primary care—supporting prevention, early detection, and the management of both acute and chronic conditions across the lifespan.

Annual physical examinations Routine check-ups and wellness visits Vaccinations and immunizations Health screenings Lifestyle counseling

Acute Illness & Injury Care

Care for common illnesses, new symptoms, minor concerns, and visits when you are not feeling well.

Common colds, flu, and acute infections Respiratory illnesses (bronchitis, pneumonia, COVID-19) Minor injuries Headaches and migraines Gastrointestinal concerns

Mental Health & Behavioral Support

Mental health care that supports emotional wellness, treatment planning, and whole-person healing.

Depression, ADHD, and anxiety screenings Medication management Referrals to therapists or psychiatric specialists when appropriate

Medication Management

Medication review, monitoring, and support to help keep your treatment safe and effective.

Evaluation for medical weight-loss therapies Prescription weight-loss medications (when appropriate) Ongoing monitoring and lifestyle support

Women's & Men's Health

Care focused on preventive wellness, routine screenings, and long-term health at every stage of life.

Gynecologic referrals Menopause care and hormone therapy management Prostate health and erectile dysfunction support STD testing and treatment Pregnancy testing and prenatal referrals

Weight Management

Supportive care focused on healthy weight goals, lifestyle changes, and sustainable progress.

Evaluation for medical weight-loss therapies Prescription weight-loss medications (when appropriate) Ongoing monitoring and lifestyle support

DOT Physical Examination

Evaluations to help commercial drivers meet federal health and safety requirements for certification.

Department of Transportation (DOT) medical exams Certified evaluations in compliance with DOT requirements

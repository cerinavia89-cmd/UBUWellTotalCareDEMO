About us

At UbuWell Total Health, we offer secure Telehealth and Video Therapy services to ensure convenient, accessible, and high-quality mental health care. Our virtual visits allow you to connect with experienced providers, therapists, and psychiatric specialists from the comfort and privacy of your home.

Our goal is to make comprehensive healthcare and mental health support more accessible, flexible, and patient-centered than ever before.

Our Vision

Our vision is to create a healthcare environment where every individual is empowered to live a healthier life within a community that values mutual respect, support, and shared responsibility for well-being. We envision UbuWell Total Health as a leading provider of holistic health services, with a focus on preventive care, behavioral health, and lifestyle management, serving patients across all generations and backgrounds.

Ubuntu Values

Our approach is guided by the values of Ubuntu: survival, solidarity, compassion, respect, and dignity.

Ubuntu Principles

We follow the core principles of Ubuntu, which include responsibility, self-control, caring, love, perseverance, honesty, compassion, and forgiveness.

Golden Rule

We follow the Golden Rule in all we do, treating others the way we want to be treated.
